* Reusable Interview Phrases
** Clarifying
- "Can I clarify the requirements first?"
- "What are the expected constraints?"
- "Should I optimize for time or memory?"

** Thinking
- "Let me think out loud."
- "My first approach would be brute force."
- "I think we can optimize this using a hash map."

** Complexity
- "Time complexity is O(n)."
- "Space complexity is O(n)."

** Uncertainty
- "I’m not fully sure, but this is how I would approach it."
- "I haven’t implemented this in production yet, but..."
